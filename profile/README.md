## About CSLabs

**CSLabs** is an independent Cybersecurity Research & Engineering Lab focused on researching, building, and sharing practical security technologies.

We explore real-world cybersecurity problems through research, experimentation, engineering, and open technical collaboration.

Our goal is simple:

> **Turn security research into practical engineering.**

## Research Areas

### Cybersecurity Engineering

Security architecture, endpoint security, detection engineering, SOC technologies, security infrastructure, and defensive security engineering.

### Data Security & Privacy

Data protection, Data Loss Prevention (DLP), data classification, privacy engineering, data governance, and secure data processing.

### AI for Cybersecurity

Large Language Models, AI agents, machine learning, intelligent security analysis, security automation, and applied AI for cybersecurity.

### Security Research

Security experimentation, prototypes, open-source technologies, technical evaluations, and applied cybersecurity research.

## Our Approach

<p align="center">
  <strong>Research → Experiment → Engineer → Test → Share</strong>
</p>

We believe useful security research should ultimately lead to better engineering.

## Projects

CSLabs develops and publishes research projects, experimental technologies, engineering tools, and open-source software.

Public projects will be available through this GitHub organization.

## AI Research

Models, datasets, and interactive AI research from CSLabs will also be published through our Hugging Face organization.

## Publications

Research notes, technical articles, engineering documentation, and other publications are available at **cslabs.vn**.
